## Replaceable reconstruction
Raw RGBA16F = indirect RGB + mean bounce hit distance (miss radius, invalid W=-1). Separate renderer-owned guides = primary call/primitive identity and clip depth, octahedral geometric normal. Identities are replay-local, not temporal.
The first backend consumes raw plus guides, performs 7x7 edge-aware taps at two-pixel spacing, rejecting invalid samples, >2.5% relative depth or normal agreement<.9. Result is separate RGBA16F RGB + confidence (valid support weight). Confidence fades composition authority at silhouettes and sparse support. Raw remains unchanged and independently viewable.
Pipeline/resources initialize before recording AS work, so failure retains raster fallback. No history or motion/reprojection is asserted. This spatial backend removes substantial noise but cannot provide strong temporal reconstruction; a future Ray Regeneration/DLSS adapter must supply any missing canonical motion/history guides and replace the backend, not game/GI/composition semantics.
